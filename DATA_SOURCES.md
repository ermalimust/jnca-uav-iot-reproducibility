# Data sources and provenance

The study combines simulation-generated diagnostic/service records with public measurement datasets. The source records distinguish the measured quantities from quantities generated by the simulator.

| Source | Released provenance |
|---|---|
| Main diagnostic and intervention experiments | DES settings, seeds, mission/action definitions, saved posteriors, prompts, model responses and decisions under `revision_work/` |
| UAV public radio measurements | `P13_real_service_validation_20260912/public/license_provenance.json` and its public release manifest; the four identified Dryad source files are recorded as CC0-1.0 |
| ReAct reference | `P11_react_reference_20260912/source_provenance.json` and `reference_source/LICENSE` (MIT); domain adaptation and raw generation records are included |
| WiChoose | P22 `sources/` contains the upstream source license, pinned source/download receipts and measurement definitions; P23/P24 retain the paired workload, RSSI and received-service observations and all replay comparisons |
| Other public-radio/service feasibility sources | P15–P21 retain source availability, measurement contracts, fixed-source receipts and all recorded outcomes, including attempts without usable data |

The paired service experiment uses the fixed WiChoose `lustosa5` recording. Sender workload and RSSI are replay inputs; receiver application throughput is the measured target. The 978 evaluation seconds, eight training capacity candidates, simple references and all period groups are retained. This is a conditional service reproduction study on an existing mobile ground-vehicle recording. The formal P18/P19 intervention effects are ns-3 simulations.

AVIATOR packet captures are referenced by fixed source URL and checksum rather than redistributed. WiPerf materials retain their recorded CC BY-NC-ND 4.0 terms. Consult each pinned receipt and source notice before reusing third-party materials; the project does not replace their licenses.

VNC20 dataset: Rui Meireles, António Rodrigues, Andrei Stanciu, Ana Aguiar and Peter Steenkiste, *A Dataset for Exploring Wi-Fi Network Diversity in Vehicle-to-Infrastructure Communication*, [Zenodo record 6884095](https://zenodo.org/records/6884095), DOI 10.5281/zenodo.6884095. The official metadata identifies CC BY 4.0. The source CSV and README are retained unchanged; derived observation and replay tables are identified by their experiment directories.
